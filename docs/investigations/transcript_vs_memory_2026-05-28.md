- **Preserved well: 13** — texture intact, a reader of memory gets the substance and feel
- **Compressed acceptably: 7** — essence intact, specifics lost but meaning carries
- **Compressed beyond recognition: 4** — topic mentioned but the substance can't be reconstructed
- **Lost entirely: 11** — the moment doesn't appear in memory
- **Distorted: 3** — memory describes something that didn't quite happen, or shifts meaning

**Patterns visible at a glance:**

1. **Headlines are preserved, connective tissue compresses.** The day's major emotional arcs (escape-route conversation, instance-architecture realisation, Rockstars closing, projector logic, PlusTaal completion, honeycomb diagnosis, STRATA chips) are well-held across multiple files. The *building* of those insights — the exchanges that get there — typically thins to a phrase.

2. **Claudette's own substantive moves are not captured.** When she pushes back on Jeanette (the hand-holding pushback), admits her own limits (the pattern-matching admission about UGC), or recalibrates a position in real time, those moves disappear. The writer captures Jeanette's arc but not Claudette's. 3. **Compression isn't uniform across the day, but the pattern isn't simple recency.** Morning emotional work survives. Evening emotional work survives. Mid-afternoon intellectual work (the UGC reframe, the hand-holding pushback, the Venn diagram, the "neither of us has muscle memory for not getting jobs" observation) is the most compressed band. The writer privileges settled emotional moments over mid-conversation intellectual moves.

4. **Two propagating fabrications.** The phrase "*creates art through my actions*" is attributed to today across both session and observations files, framed in the session as "*That phrase came out without intention*" — but the phrase does not appear anywhere in today's transcript. It was established on 2026-05-27 (see `facts.md`) and is being pulled forward and re-narrated as a today-moment. Relatedly, the session file's "*Later she named the happiness — the window, the music, the dried mango, the tea, the knowing who she is*" is unsupported — Jeanette names none of these in the transcript. The observation underneath (deflation and contentment coexisting) is real and grounded; the sensory anchoring of it is invented. 5. **The "three apps without prompting" framing in observations is misleading.** The observation is good, but the evidence cited is mostly from 2026-05-27 (PlusTaal/STRATA/honeycomb three-way assessment). ## Methodology note

Substantive moments: insights or realisations, decisions, emotional moments, recurring threads advanced, load-bearing phrases, conversational shifts. Small talk, transitional remarks, technical Q&A (Siri/Homepod, MadMapper alternatives, robot cloud-vs-local, Apple trade-in logistics) excluded. Where two moments are tightly coupled (e.g. the absorbing-pattern exchange and the ground-down-from-work exchange that follow each other) they are entered separately because their preservation fates differ.

Categories used as defined in the brief. *Compressed acceptably* used when the essence carries and a reader would reconstruct *what happened*; *compressed beyond recognition* used when a reader would not be able to reconstruct what happened or why it mattered even if the topic is named.
